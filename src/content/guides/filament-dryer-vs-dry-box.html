An active dryer and a dry storage box solve different jobs. Choose according to whether filament needs moisture removal, storage after drying, or protection during a long print. A low humidity display is not by itself evidence that the entire spool has completed an appropriate drying cycle.

Start with the material and task

Task Evidence to seek
Dry a moisture-affected spool Exact filament guidance and equipment capable of the specified cycle
Store already-dry filament Sealed storage, maintained desiccant and spool fit
Feed during a long print Feed path, spool rotation and the equipment's stated operating mode

Prusa's drying guidance provides product-specific starting information. Follow the exact formulation and spool limits, rather than inventing one temperature for everything called PETG or nylon.

Keep a source-reading record for the exact spool

  1. Identify product, PA chemistry or TPU formulation, filler and document revision. A family label does not select the cycle.
  2. Find the product's actual drying instruction and when it applies. Keep the spool/container temperature limits alongside it.
  3. Verify the dryer's operating range and allowed mode. A sealed storage box does not establish active-drying capability.
  4. Check spool rotation, outlets and the permitted feed arrangement while printing. Record drying date and subsequent exposure separately.

Keep later humidity, temperature, exposure duration and fit readings separate; use conditioned-part measurements when fit changes after printing, rather than choosing another drying cycle from a dimensional percentage.

An equipment decision without invented compatibility

For an active dryer, verify temperature control, cycle guidance, spool envelope, number of spools and whether printing while the machine operates is supported. Worked investigation: stringing after storage

Illustrative workflow: a PETG spool starts stringing after a long storage period. Record its prior settings and the exact product. Check the manufacturer's drying instructions; if a drying cycle is appropriate, compare the same model and profile afterward. Persistent stringing can also involve temperature, travel, retraction, contamination or geometry. Do not declare the dryer ineffective or moisture the sole cause from one uncontrolled print.

Use the material hub for formulation caveats and the spool planner when weighing the remaining job stock. This page is a documented decision framework, not a hands-on equipment test or ranked shopping list.

Record spool and feeding constraints

Measure the actual spool envelope, including flange diameter and width, before choosing equipment. Check the manufacturer's supported spool count, bearing or roller arrangement, outlet routing and operating modes. For a long nylon job, a sealed feed path can matter after an appropriate drying cycle, but the box cannot choose that cycle. Keep the exact filament instructions and equipment limitations with the print profile.
