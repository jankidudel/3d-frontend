Build a print quote from the actual work and an explicit profit method. Material, machine time, preparation, finishing, failure allowance and minimum order all need visible assumptions. A calculator can expose cost and margin; it cannot tell you what customers will pay.

Use the editable price calculator with your chosen currency. GBP, USD, CAD, AUD, NZD or EUR labels do not convert prices or identify tax obligations. All figures below are hypothetical currency units (CU), not market rates.

Price the shared fictional job

The five-part, two-plate production record has K = 39.192 CU. Enter or explicitly apply that complete order cost in the price calculator. Job-total mode does not multiply K by quantity or add another production failure budget. Any entered quantity remains a planned count; mixed parts stay at order level.

Choose margin or markup explicitly

Without fees, 30% target margin gives P = K / (1 - m) = 39.192 / 0.70 = 55.988571… CU, displayed 55.99. A 30% markup gives P = K × (1 + u) = 50.9496 CU, displayed 50.95. Percentages become fractions. Margin is profit divided by price; markup in this implementation targets profit u × K after entered fees.

Declare the exact selling-fee basis

For fixed fee F once per order and percentage f on the calculator selling price P, margin mode uses P = (K + F) / (1 - m - f). With m = 0.30, f = 0.05 and F = 0, P is 60.295384… CU, displayed 60.30. Require m + f < 1. Markup mode uses P = (K × (1 + u) + F) / (1 - f); 30% markup and a 5% fee give 53.631157… CU, displayed 53.63. Require f < 1.

This simplified fee scenario is implemented, but it is not a marketplace tax/shipping model. Do not import a hardcoded fee table. If fees are already incorporated in K, mark that basis and leave separate fees at zero. Tax and unentered costs remain excluded; currency selection changes labels without conversion.

Minimum price changes realised margin

Apply the minimum order price after the chosen method. With a 65 CU minimum, the fee-adjusted 60.295384… CU result becomes 65 CU. At the entered 5% fee, fees are 3.25 CU; profit is 65 - 3.25 - 39.192 = 22.558 CU and realised margin is 34.704615…%. The target remains 30%; the realised margin changes. The calculator recomputes these outputs after the minimum.

Full precision is kept in calculations and JSON. Rounded unit values need not multiply to the rounded order total. Do not quote mixed parts as if a single average were their individual cost.

Make the inputs editable and retain the assumptions

  1. Slice the agreed revision, material and orientation; copy complete material and duration estimates.
  2. Set your currency, actual material cost, machine allowance and hands-on labour.
  3. Add delivery, fees and tax treatment explicitly where relevant. Estimates are not invoices or tax advice.
  4. Choose margin or markup deliberately, then apply your stated minimum-order policy.
  5. Export the quote assumptions and retain the file-review report alongside the approved revision.

Share setup only when it is shared

Record once-per-order work in the production job. Independent runs repeat handling even if preparation was shared. Re-slice changed quantity or layout; the batch guide owns those allocation decisions. An agreed minimum-order policy is a price rule, separate from that cost scope.

Failure allowance and minimum order

Use a fixed budget or an observed retry model and document which costs it covers. Do not add an expected failure cost again if the entered machine rate already includes it. With credible comparable-job data, dividing eligible production cost by a success fraction is one scenario; it is not a predicted failure rate for unfamiliar geometry.

A minimum order can cover administration that a small part still needs. In a hypothetical policy with a 15 CU minimum, a calculated 9 CU quote becomes 15 CU; a 32.86 CU quote remains 32.86 CU. State this policy to the customer and avoid counting the same setup twice.

Quote only after intake questions are resolved

Record quantity, exact material/colour, functional dimensions, included design changes, finish, delivery date and quote expiry. A customer-supplied file with unresolved units or missing features needs intake clarification before an unconditional production quote. A clean mesh report does not price support removal or establish material suitability.

Recalculate when quantity, process, revision or orientation changes. Use the production-cost explanation for the underlying inputs, then build your quote using your own values.

Price an agreed coupon or fit-validation iteration

Offer a representative coupon or prototype when it resolves an agreed functional requirement. Define the tested feature, counterpart, process/profile, post-processing, measurement method and acceptance evidence in customer intake. A clean mesh is not assembly acceptance, and an iteration is not a promised successful fit.

Record one-time design/preparation as setup, coupon production as its own material/machine work, and measuring, installation or finishing as hands-on labour. If setup is already included, do not add it again to every coupon. State the included iteration count or separately agreed scope; revised CAD or a new profile can require additional work. The existing quote worksheet retains those assumptions, while the measurement worksheet records the fit calculation and user-entered outcome. No universal prototype price follows from these calculators.

Record a quote worksheet after agreeing the work

Use the price calculator and printable worksheet to separate once-per-order setup and packaging from per-part labour and finishing. Choose a currency label, quantity and margin explicitly. Avoid counting failure allowance twice if your machine rate already includes it. The export retains calculation inputs locally until you deliberately download or print it; it is an estimate, not an invoice or production acceptance.

Prepare the customer quotation

Once you have reviewed the selling price, use the quotation generator to prepare the customer document. Include the job quantity, material, finish, model revision, delivery scope and validity date. Keep your internal cost and margin worksheet separately. Downloading a quotation does not record customer acceptance.

If the customer changes the job, retain the previous quotation and review the new specification before pricing again. A changed quantity is not automatically a proportional change in production cost.
