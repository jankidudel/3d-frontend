Check whether triangle edges have a neighbour. An exposed edge belongs to only one face and marks an open boundary under this mesh check. A result of zero open edges supports closure, but it does not prove correct design, non-intersection or printability. Use the STL Watertight Checker to inspect your exact file.

Establish the meaning with two controls

The closed 20-unit cube has 12 triangles and zero boundary edges. The open-face copy deletes two triangles and has four boundary edges. Under a millimetre assumption, local preflight screens the closed cube's volume at 8,000 mm³; volume is unavailable for the open copy. STL itself contains no length unit. Recheck both controls before interpreting a report on a more complex design.

Interpret closure alongside other findings

Result What it establishes Next check
Open edges above zero At least one boundary is exposed under the checker's coordinate and edge rules. Locate the loop and compare it with intended design; choose whether to patch.
Zero open edges, one shell No boundary was found by this method. Check orientation, non-manifold edges, intersections, dimensions and sliced layers.
Zero open edges, several shells Each surface may be closed, but the file has separate components. Identify intended parts, nested shells and overlaps; do not delete them by count alone.
Malformed parse or check unavailable No valid zero finding exists. Re-export the source and rerun the check.

A thick-walled vessel's inner and outer walls can meet around an open mouth and still form a closed material surface. A single uncapped sheet has exposed edges. A hidden internal shell or self-intersection can remain even with zero open edges. The broader preflight reports other supported checks and their unavailable or sampled states.

Recheck the file you will slice

  1. Keep the original and record the location and count of the reported boundary.
  2. Repair only a surface whose intended shape is known, preferably in source CAD for functional geometry.
  3. Open the saved derivative, rerun closure and topology checks, then inspect the affected slicer layers and dimensions.

Watertightness is one property of a surface. It does not measure wall thickness, support, printer fit, material behaviour or the outcome of a physical print.
