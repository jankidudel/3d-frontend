Work out a starting flow value from a single-wall test

Flow rate and e-steps both affect extrusion, but they are not interchangeable. Treating them as the same can create a profile that works for one spool and fails when material changes.

E-steps or rotation distance: printer-level

This setting describes the relationship between extruder motor motion and filament movement. If it is substantially wrong, every profile starts from the wrong extrusion amount. Follow the process for your firmware and inspect mechanical causes such as a slipping drive gear.

Flow rate: slicer-level

Flow, also called extrusion multiplier, is a small correction in a slicer profile. It accounts for a particular filament, nozzle, line width, temperature and speed. PLA, PETG and different nozzle sizes can need different values.

Which comes first?

First make sure extrusion hardware and machine-level calibration are sane. Then tune flow per material/profile. If a calculation suggests an unusually large change, check line width, nozzle condition and measurement method instead of accepting it blindly.

Neither fixes every fit issue

Correct flow does not guarantee a nominal hole or decide a sliding gap. Use the Tolerance & Clearance Calculator for mating dimensions and the Shrinkage Calculator for consistent whole-model axis error.

Diagnose before changing either value

Symptom Check first
Every material under-extrudes similarly Filament path, drive gear, nozzle and extruder calibration
One material shows thin walls Temperature, moisture, line-width preview and a material-specific flow test
Only corners or seams look wrong Pressure/linear-advance behaviour, acceleration and cooling

E-steps describe commanded extruder travel; flow is a profile-level multiplier used by the slicer. Do not use a flow multiplier to hide a mechanical feed problem, and do not change firmware extruder calibration to correct one unusual filament. Print a controlled single-wall or manufacturer-recommended test, measure several positions, make a small documented adjustment, then repeat.
