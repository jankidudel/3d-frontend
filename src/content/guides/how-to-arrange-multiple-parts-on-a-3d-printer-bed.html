Choose the usable region and independent allowances before judging how many parts belong on a plate. A compact arrangement is useful only when its actual sliced structures and printing risks are acceptable. Keep the accepted job count separate from all uploaded files and from successful production.

Choose the usable region and active source

In the bed layout planner, choose a confirmed saved profile or explicitly use editable session settings. A saved inch or centimetre profile is converted to millimetres; it does not silently become the starter bed. X/Y span the bed and Z is checked separately. Review ambiguous legacy axes before saving. Session edits control the active session bed; saved-mode edits apply after explicit confirmation and save.

Choose Rectangle or Circle deliberately. The circular-envelope guide explains why a rectangle narrower than the diameter can still put corners outside. Irregular exclusions and height-dependent machine regions remain native-profile questions.

Set independent allowances

Bed edge exclusion removes each rectangular edge or a radial band on a circle. Extra XY allowance per part expands the model envelope. Inter-part spacing is measured between those expanded rectangles. Do not apply one physical allowance twice. Use the process-space budgeting guide for worked body-gap and expansion examples; a uniform reservation is not generated brim, support or purge geometry.

The earlier rectangular control remains reproducible: on a 220 x 220 mm bed with 5 mm edge exclusion, 5 mm inter-part spacing and zero expansion, two 100 x 80 mm envelopes can start at x=5 and x=110, y=5. The second ends at x=210, leaving 10 mm at the right boundary. These are illustrative planning coordinates, not a machine origin or a universal gap requirement.

Interpret the suggested placement

Rectangle mode uses a shelf arrangement; circle mode tries centred, adjacent and bounded grid candidates with optional 90-degree turns. Every accepted reserved rectangle is inside the stated region and respects the selected gap. The finite heuristic does not search every arrangement or nest arbitrary outlines. Not placed by this layout means no accepted candidate was found, not proof that another plate is necessary. Try a different arrangement, rotation, lower count or native slicer.

The numbered diagram and text table report reserved rectangle positions in the planner frame. Area use is accepted model-envelope or reserved-rectangle area divided by effective region area, using a disc for circles. It is not actual bed contact, deposited material or optimal nesting density.

Review the native generated plate

  1. Use the orientation planner to compare six directions, then apply the intended rotation in the slicer.
  2. Keep compatible material, layer and support requirements together; review object-level overrides.
  3. Generate adhesion structures, support bases and purge/wipe structures, then inspect all their extents and relevant travel.
  4. For one-at-a-time printing, perform the separate head/gantry and order checks. Nonoverlapping envelopes do not certify sequential clearance.
  5. Review the whole slice and save the native project and exact profile.

Choose the failure domain deliberately

Several parts share a failure domain: a detached object or failed support can spoil otherwise valid neighbours. Group stable, inexpensive parts when settings agree, and consider separate jobs for tall, delicate or critical items. Cooling and repeated travel also change across a multi-part plate. A full bed's nominal throughput does not establish lower expected loss or better surface quality.

Do not respond to every unplaced rectangle by shrinking functional parts or weakening adhesion settings. First check shape, orientation, actual requirements and alternative arrangements. Individual model fit remains distinct from this multi-part placement task.

Record the accepted job

Confirm only the accepted placement count before offering it to the cost calculator. Changing parts, profile, shape, units, edge exclusion, spacing or expansion clears that confirmation. The recipient requires a deliberate apply action and preserves existing job rows. The offered count is unsliced; it supplies no successful-production count, grams or duration.

Slice the accepted plate and enter complete consumption and time in the production-cost workflow. Record quantity, orientation, usable-region basis, allowances, profile and unresolved machine checks together. Keep rejected suggestions separate from accepted work and repeat the review after any material change.
