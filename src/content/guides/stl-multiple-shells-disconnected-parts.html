Separate surface groups can be intended parts, fragments or overlapping bodies. Identify what belongs before choosing a connectivity method or removing anything. A component count depends on the method; it cannot establish a complete delivery or a Boolean union.

Identify intended pieces

List the source revision and required parts, then open the complete geometry. Keep a piece when it belongs to the intended model, even if it is small. Remove it only after its role is understood. If the design needs intersecting bodies to become one solid, return to the source model; choosing which pieces to retain does not perform a Boolean union.

In a print-in-place mechanism, a small captive pin or moving link can be an essential separate body. Keeping only the largest body can delete it and disable the mechanism. Identify every intended body before selection; separation under either connectivity method supplies no minimum-gap, release or movement result.

Separate parts are not evidence of fragments; confirm the design before changing the retained set.

Distinguish contact, overlap and slicer regions

Overlaps need another question

The overlapping cube example contains two independently closed surfaces that cross. A connectivity check can report two shells and no over-shared edges while the surfaces still intersect. Summing individual enclosed volumes double-counts the overlap unless a correct union is established. InspectMesh's collection check does not perform a Boolean union or exhaustive self-intersection check.

Near-coincident coordinates need not weld; exact contact does not prove a valid manufactured assembly. The slicer-region counterexample shows why unsupported-layer review is distinct from counting disconnected mesh groups. Intersections and nested-solid interpretation remain unsupported in the collection screen.

Select a justified next action

  1. Record intended parts, unit assumption and the report method.
  2. Inspect every group and check boundary, winding and over-shared-edge findings separately.
  3. Use STL Repair piece selection only for already separate geometry. Every piece starts kept; Keep largest part is a reversible surface-area proposal, with a confirmation for similarly sized leading pieces.
  4. Use the split workflow only when a new cutting seam is intended. A design union belongs in source CAD.
  5. Download a separate selected copy. Kept triangles retain their coordinates and winding; removing an outer fragment can change whole-file bounds without moving a kept part. Compare that exact revision and inspect the receiving slicer.
