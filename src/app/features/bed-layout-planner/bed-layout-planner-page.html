Footprints, spacing and rejected placements

This browser-local planner packs axis-aligned rectangular XY footprints, with optional 90-degree turns, using a conservative shelf arrangement. STL coordinates are treated as millimetres; 3MF units and supported build transforms are resolved. It does not nest arbitrary outlines or search every packing arrangement.

For a 220 x 220 mm bed and 5 mm margin, two 100 x 80 mm footprints can occupy x = 5 and x = 110, with y = 5. The second ends at x = 210, leaving 10 mm to the right edge. A 10 mm-wide brim on each part exceeds the 5 mm inter-part gap; reserve more space and verify the sliced footprints.

Rejected placements need another batch or a different layout. A suggested fit does not certify sequential printing: toolhead clearance, gantry collisions, supports, exclusions and Z heights belong in the slicer/printer review. Failed file processing is separate from an empty selection.
