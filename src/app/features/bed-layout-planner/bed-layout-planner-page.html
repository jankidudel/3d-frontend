Footprints, spacing and rejected placements

This browser-local planner packs axis-aligned rectangular XY footprints, with optional 90-degree turns, on rectangles or circular usable regions. STL coordinates are treated as millimetres; 3MF units and supported build transforms are resolved. It does not nest arbitrary outlines or search every packing arrangement.

For a 220 x 220 mm bed and 5 mm margin, two 100 x 80 mm footprints can occupy x = 5 and x = 110, with y = 5. The second ends at x = 210, leaving 10 mm to the right edge. A 10 mm-wide brim on each part exceeds the 5 mm inter-part gap; reserve more space and verify the sliced footprints. The packed rectangle reserves space. The native preview shows the actual brim paths. If one copy shows loose lines during deposition, record its ID and bed position and check the affected region. If you try a new position, keep the copy's identity and review the full regenerated job, including its brims and print order.

A part not placed by this layout may work in a different arrangement. A suggested fit does not certify sequential printing: toolhead clearance, gantry collisions, supports, exclusions and Z heights belong in the slicer/printer review. Failed file processing is separate from an empty selection.

Record the actual sliced plate

Slice the chosen layout in your printer's slicer. Enter its complete material consumption and duration in Several plates / one job. Rectangular placement supplies neither grams nor print time and does not establish successful production.

Open Several plates / one job at the destination, then add the offered unsliced plate. Existing job rows are preserved.

Model placement versus sliced motion

This tool screens rectangular model footprints. Sliced positive-extrusion and travel extents can include purge/prime moves, start/end routines and Z hops. Review their explicit coordinate minima/maxima in the G-code viewer and coordinate-scope guide. For sequential printing use native head/gantry clearance review; a fitted footprint is insufficient.
