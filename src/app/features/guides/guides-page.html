Orientation, overhangs and support access

Choose an orientation from the requirement that matters most before confirming the actual layers in the target slicer.

  • Lay an imported STL flat deliberately - separate face alignment from final bed placement.
  • Distinguish a bridge from an overhang - inspect anchors and the first affected layer.
  • Plan support access inside holes and channels - confirm the support can leave without damaging a critical face.

After slicing: follow the finding
  • Small path outside the bed: review location and travel
  • Received another printer's G-code: record project/profile provenance
  • Incomplete preview: identify unsupported effects or import failures
  • One object at a time: review head clearance and order
