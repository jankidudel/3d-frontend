Method and next steps

STL unit assumptions and 3MF build transforms are recorded. Exact-coordinate welding precedes edge incidence, winding and connected-shell checks. Sampled wall rays review triangle centroids; they can miss thin features and do not replace a slicer. Fit uses nominal printer boxes at right angles, without brims, clips or excluded bed regions.

Review findings in STL preflight or 3MF preflight. Use STL revision comparison when checking replacement exports.
