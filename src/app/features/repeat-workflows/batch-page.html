Frequently asked collection questions

Is a skipped check a clean result?

No. Fit without a printer profile and unsupported analyses stay explicitly skipped. Self intersections are not checked; compare connectivity and intersection checks.

Why is volume unavailable?

Open boundaries, winding or multiple-shell interpretation can prevent a supported solid-volume result. Check units and volume validity before substituting a number.

What happens after navigation or reload?

Internal handoffs retain the selected file and collection in this tab's memory. Reloading requires reselection. Clear the collection to release its original files. Saving report history is optional and stores findings only.

Can I review a revised file?

Use Replace this file on its result card. The recorded collection settings are retained and other completed results remain. A new ordinary collection selection starts a new queue.

Method and next steps

Interpret separate shells before deleting or merging parts.

STL unit assumptions and 3MF build transforms are recorded. Exact-coordinate welding precedes edge incidence, winding and connected-shell checks. Sampled wall rays review triangle centroids; they can miss thin features and do not replace a slicer. Fit uses nominal printer boxes at right angles, without brims, clips or excluded bed regions.

Review findings in STL preflight or 3MF preflight. Use STL revision comparison when checking replacement exports.
