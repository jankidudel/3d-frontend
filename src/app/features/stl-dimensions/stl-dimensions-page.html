STL measurement and scaling

Measure, convert and scale STL dimensions

Find your model’s width, height and depth, interpret its design unit, and calculate a proportional scale for the size you want to print.

STL geometry is stored as unitless coordinates, so STL files do not reliably contain unit metadata. You choose how those coordinates should be interpreted after measurement.

How STL sizing works

An STL records vertex coordinates but normally does not say whether a value of 50 means 50 millimetres, centimetres, metres or inches. Select the unit used by the design software, then use the conversions and proportional scale calculator above to prepare the intended print size.
