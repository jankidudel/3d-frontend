How to calculate a 3D printing price

Add material, power, machine wear, labour, order overhead and a realistic failure allowance before applying a margin. The worked defaults are illustrative only.

Cost vs price vs profit margin

Cost is what producing the item consumes. Price is what the customer pays. Profit margin is profit divided by price, after the costs entered here.

Frequently asked questions

How much should I charge for a 3D print?

Use your real costs and choose a margin that fits your work and market; a calculator cannot determine market demand.

Does this include electricity and failed prints?

Yes, when you enter electricity and failure allowance values.

Does the price include shipping and tax?

No. Add only costs you intend to include in the fixed order overhead, and handle tax separately where required.
