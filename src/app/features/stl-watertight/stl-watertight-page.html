How it works

The checker examines how triangles meet. Open edges indicate an unsealed boundary, while non-manifold edges can make a closed volume ambiguous.

What the results mean

Watertight means the mesh forms a closed surface. Separate shells can be intentional, but should be checked in your design.

FAQ

Can a watertight model still fail to print?

Yes. Wall thickness, supports, material and printer setup are separate concerns.

What should I do with open edges?

Repair the mesh in your modelling or mesh-repair software, then check it again.

Use this check when you need to find exposed triangle boundaries. In the open-face cube, a missing square face leaves four open edges. The opening may be intentional: inspect its location before accepting a repair that changes the design.

The closed 20 mm control has zero detected boundaries and 8,000 mm³ screened volume. Volume on the open example is unavailable, not zero. Even no detected open edges cannot establish a valid solid when orientation, intersections or overlapping shells remain unresolved.

Files are uploaded for server inspection. Use the manifold checker for ambiguous edge connections and shell interpretation, then confirm any changed export. Watertightness does not determine supports or successful printing.
