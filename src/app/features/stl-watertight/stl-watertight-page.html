Closed-surface check

STL Watertight Checker

Find out whether your STL is a closed surface or has holes. This checker answers one question: are there any open edges, and how many? Use it when a slicer reports holes or a non-solid model. For a full list of mesh problems, use the STL Quality Checker instead. Zero open edges means no holes were found; it does not decide whether an opening was meant to be there.

Example: a closed cube and an open cube

The 20 mm cube with one face removed has 10 triangles and four open edges; the closed 20 mm cube has 12 triangles, no open edges and a volume of 8,000 mm³ when opened as millimetres. The open cube has no volume, which is not the same as zero. See our saved test results (run on our code locally, not the live server).

In the example, the open edges outline the missing side. In your own model, decide whether an opening is a mistake: a designed hole or channel is surrounded by closed walls and has no open edges, so don't fill it just because it looks open. Use the watertight guide to decide. Check for other mesh problems if holes aren't the only question, or use the manifold checker for non-manifold warnings.

How the check works

The server matches edges by exact corner positions; nearby points are not merged. Zero-area and invalid triangles are skipped, but duplicate triangles are counted. An edge used by one triangle is an open edge (a hole); an edge used by three or more is a separate non-manifold problem. Pieces sharing a corner count as connected, even if that would not hold together when printed. It does not check corners for non-manifold problems or look for overlapping surfaces.

"No open edges", "open edges found" and "could not be checked" are three different results. Volume can be blank for other reasons, such as the model being several separate pieces; a blank volume does not mean the model has holes.

How to fix a hole

If a surface really is missing, rebuild it in your CAD program or a mesh editor; see how to fix holes in an STL. InspectMesh cleanup only removes existing triangles and cannot create a missing face. Reopen the saved copy, check it again, and look at the sliced layers around the repair.
