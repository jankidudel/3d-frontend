How to use an available volume

3MF declares a coordinate unit; supported transforms resolve placed geometry before measurement. The current method withholds volume for multiple connected components. A count or dimensions result does not establish that an enclosed-volume value is available. Optional solid mass multiplies an available volume in cm³ by your density in g/cm³ locally; it does not predict sliced consumption.

Worked build and density example

Illustrative arithmetic: two non-overlapping 20 mm cubes contain 16 cm³ in total. At a chosen density of 1.24 g/cm³, their solid mass is 19.84 g. This is not a claim that this endpoint reports a value for every two-item build, and it is not sliced filament consumption.

Declared inches convert linearly by 25.4 to millimetres, and volume converts by 25.4³. A positive surface sum does not establish a Boolean union of overlapping shells or the intended meaning of a cavity.
