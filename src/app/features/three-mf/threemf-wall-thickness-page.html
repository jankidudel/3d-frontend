Measure the placed build, not just an unplaced resource

This local analysis resolves supported 3MF build items and component transforms into a combined triangle scene, then converts the declared coordinate unit. Repeated mesh resources contribute their actual placed instances. It analyses the resolved build together; it does not offer an individual-instance selection control on this page. Confirm that the part you care about is present and oriented as intended.

The declared-inch cube example has one-coordinate-unit spans: its resolved size is 25.4 mm on each axis. Its translation changes placement, not wall thickness. An STL unit selector elsewhere must not override this package's declared unit. Unsupported units or unresolved geometry produce an error rather than a guessed thickness.

Interpret gaps and multiple instances carefully

Centroid normal rays provide bounded samples, with up to 2,000 selected triangles. A hit in another nearby body is not necessarily the wall you intended to measure. Separate parts, overlaps and assembly gaps therefore require visual review. No-hit samples remain unmeasured; a result with unmeasured samples is not an all-clear wall report.

Confirm the selected display unit and review threshold, inspect the highlighted locations and compare important walls with source CAD. This does not interpret application-specific slicer profiles, validate supports or certify a process minimum. Stop if the build differs from the source or an important instance is absent. Resolve missing build objects before sampling them, and retain the original package.
