Placed-build interpretation

The checker resolves supported mesh build items, repeated resources and nested component transforms into one millimetre scene. It samples at most 2,000 selected triangle centroids; unmeasured rays and unsampled triangles remain outside the measured result. Open boundaries, neighbouring parts and assembly gaps need review in the original application. Unsupported required or recommended extensions, unsafe packages and unresolved mesh geometry stop with an error rather than a guessed result.

Read the sampling method. Keep the original 3MF and compare critical geometry with its native slicer preview.
