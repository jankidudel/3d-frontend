A complete illustrative production subtotal

For a sliced job using 100 g from a 1,000 g spool costing £20, material is £2. Five hours at an assumed average 120 W and £0.25/kWh adds £0.15. Fifteen minutes of active work at £12/hour adds £3. The subtotal is £5.15 with machine allocation, extra parts, packaging, shipping and failure allowance set to zero. These are illustrative inputs, including the power assumption, rather than measured printer consumption or a recommended labour rate. Select GBP to reproduce this display; other currencies use the rates you enter without conversion.

A 10% failure allowance adds £0.515 to this subtotal, giving £5.665 before display rounding. The calculator applies the percentage to all entered production and overhead costs, including packaging and shipping. It is a budgeting uplift, not a statistical expected-retry model. Do not add material waste twice if the slicer total already includes it.

Next, choose margin and fees in the price calculator. Once the customer charge is agreed, prepare a quotation with its scope and revision. Neither profit nor sales tax belongs in this production subtotal.
