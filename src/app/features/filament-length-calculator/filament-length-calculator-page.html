What this estimate includes

The calculation treats filament as a solid cylinder. It is useful for comparing spool labels, planning stock and translating slicer estimates. Actual spool length can vary with diameter tolerance, density, additives and manufacturer specification. Use the value printed on your spool when it differs. Enter an independently established filament length, not the last E coordinate: E mode, resets and retraction recovery prevent that coordinate from establishing consumed length.

Reproduce an illustrative conversion

Choose 1,000 g and an illustrative density of 1.24 g/cm³. This is an editable example input, not a universal PLA value or a measured spool.

Area = π × (diameter / 2)² mm². Volume = mass / density cm³. Converting cm³ to mm³ and mm to metres cancels the factor of 1,000, so length (m) = mass / (density × area).

For 1.75 mm filament: 1,000 / (1.24 × π × 0.875²) = 335.3 m. For 2.85 mm at the same mass/density: 126.4 m. In reverse, 335.3 m at 1.75 mm is about 1,000.1 g after rounding.

The result describes solid filament. For complete job consumption, include support and purge and use the partial-spool planner.
