Establish physical size first

For STL, confirm a known dimension under the intended unit assumption. For 3MF, check declared units and the placed-build envelope. An arrangement's total bounds need not be the size of one part.

How fit is calculated

The checker compares resolved millimetre dimensions with the selected usable build volume after subtracting your margin. Automatic mode tests only axis-aligned, right-angle permutations; it does not claim arbitrary-angle optimisation.

Why a slicer can still reject a model

Nominal build volume can differ from the usable region. Multi-colour purge towers, support space, sequential-print clearance and manufacturer exclusions can require more room than a bounding box.
