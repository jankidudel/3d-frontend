Interpret circular envelope fit and budget space added after the model.

Establish physical size first

For STL, confirm a known dimension under the intended unit assumption. For 3MF, check declared units and the placed-build envelope. An arrangement's total bounds need not be the size of one part.

How fit is calculated

The checker compares resolved millimetre X/Y/Z spans with the chosen usable rectangle or circle. Circular mode checks all corners of a centred XY envelope; an outside empty corner does not prove an irregular outline cannot fit. A circle plus Z assumes a simple cylindrical region, not height-dependent machine reach. Automatic mode tests only axis-aligned, right-angle permutations; it does not claim arbitrary-angle optimisation.

Why a slicer can still reject a model

Nominal build volume can differ from the usable region. Multi-colour purge towers, support space, sequential-print clearance and manufacturer exclusions can require more room than a bounding box.
