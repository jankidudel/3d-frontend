About InspectMesh

InspectMesh develops tools for inspecting 3D-print files and planning print jobs. File checks describe the geometry and settings they examine; they do not certify a physical print. Messages through this form provide a direct way to reach the site maintainer about a result, feature or correction.

Report an error or unclear result

Include the page address, the result you expected and what you observed. For a published example, include its filename or example name. You do not need to send your own model to report a wording problem. Keep confidential geometry out of your message.

Software examples demonstrate specific checks. Printer and material pages distinguish sourced guidance from physical testing. A source date records when a reference was checked; it does not establish current stock or price.

How can we help?

Messages are used to respond to your enquiry and improve InspectMesh. Please do not include 3D files, private design details or payment details.
