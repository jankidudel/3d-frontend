What this planner can and cannot decide

It compares +Z, -Z, +X, -X, +Y and -Y as source-axis-up directions. The +Z candidate preserves the source rotation. It does not test every face or arbitrary angle, generate supports, simulate bridge paths, predict warping or calculate strength. Confirm the selected candidate in the intended slicer, printer, material and profile.

Compare candidates, then choose for the actual job

The local planner exposes each supported metric rather than compressing them into an overall score. STL uses your unit assumption; supported 3MF build geometry is resolved to physical millimetres before analysis.

Keep intended strength, surface finish and support removal as separate decisions. Review the selected candidate in your slicer and include every brim, support and machine allowance. See the orientation procedure for the full trade-off.
