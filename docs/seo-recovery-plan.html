InspectMesh SEO recovery plan

Summary

The technical setup is mostly sound. The problem is scale and quality. A domain about three months old has published 221 indexable URLs. Many of them overlap in intent, follow one template, carry little crawlable text, or lead with caveats instead of answers. That combination usually produces large "Crawled – currently not indexed" and "Discovered – currently not indexed" counts in Search Console.

221indexable URLs in the sitemap
~100 daysfrom first commit (2026-08-12) to now
~33URLs in 3 heavily overlapping intent clusters
16tool pages with under 400 words of main content
28guides with ≤ 2 contextual inbound links
6.2average commits per guide (rewrites)

Approach (decided 2026-11-19): keep the existing pages and expand them. Where two pages compete for the same query, one keeps the head keyword and the other is retargeted to a different, more specific keyword.

Search Console data was not available for this audit, so the exact exclusion reason per URL is not confirmed. The first step of the plan is to export it.

Findings

Ranked by expected impact on indexing. Severity reflects how many URLs are affected and how directly the issue maps to known Google exclusion causes.

# Severity Area Finding Evidence Recommended fix
1 High Publishing scale Too many URLs too quickly for a new, low-authority domain. Google indexes only part of such a site until it earns trust, and the rest waits as "Discovered/Crawled – not indexed". First commit 2026-08-12. Guides added: 13 in Aug, 49 in Sep, 44 in Oct, 21 in Nov. Sitemap: 128 guides, 50 tools, 31 printer/comparison pages, 9 material pages. Freeze new URLs. Make each existing page clearly distinct and stronger (findings 2–6) instead of adding more. Resume at a slow cadence only once the index ratio improves.
2 High Cannibalisation Several URLs answer the same search intent. Google picks one and drops the others as duplicates or low value. STL errors cluster about 15 URLs, units/scale about 8, cost/price/quote about 10. See Keyword map. Keep the pages. Give each one a distinct primary keyword: the owner keeps the head term, the others are retargeted to a different query (new title, H1, description, opening and anchor text) and their overlapping passages are removed. See keyword map.
3 High Thin STL/3MF twin tools 12 tools exist as STL and 3MF pairs (24 pages). Many have very little crawlable text beyond the upload widget. Main-content words: 3mf-overhang-checker 186, stl-triangle-counter 193, 3mf-triangle-counter 215, stl-overhang-checker 229, 3mf-wall-thickness-checker 266, stl-scale-tool 297. Keep both formats and expand each with real format-specific content: worked examples, result interpretation, and what differs between STL and 3MF (3MF build items, components, transforms, multi-object builds). Where the pair competes for one query, retarget the 3MF page to a 3MF-specific query.
4 High Templated printer pages The printer spec pages are near-duplicates of each other and repeat information already published by manufacturers and review sites. 10 printer pages share 51–70% of their 5-word phrases (A1 vs A1 Mini: 70%). Each says "specification research, not a hands-on review". Zero contextual links from non-hub pages. Either expand them with content only InspectMesh has (for example fit-checker results on sample models per printer), or noindex them and drop them from the sitemap until they do. Decision still open.
5 Med-High Writing style Pages lead with caveats instead of answers. Readers and Google's helpfulness signals both read this as low value. Across guides and tools: "does not" 534×, "not establish" 162×, "physical" 306×, "observ…" 232×. Example opening: "An STL error report describes a finding, not the correction your design needs." Answer first in plain words in the first 2–3 sentences. Move limitations to one "Limits of this check" section. Cut repeated disclaimers.
6 Med-High Titles Titles drifted away from the words people search for, hurting relevance and click-through. how-to-tell-if-a-3mf-file-is-manifold → "3MF Topology: Mesh Fault or Build Placement?"; how-to-make-an-stl-file-printable → "Prepare an STL: Repair and Verify a Saved Copy"; how-to-estimate-filament-weight-from-a-3mf-file → "Estimate Solid-Model Mass from a 3MF". 25 titles exceed 65 characters. Put the query wording first ("How to Tell If a 3MF File Is Manifold"), keep under ~60 characters, then the brand.
7 Medium Constant rewrites Pages change before Google has settled on them. This also breaks the 28-day evaluation rule in docs/SEO_STRATEGY.md. 6.2 commits per guide on average (max 17). 42 guide-changing commits since 2026-10-01. About 145 sitemap lastmod dates fall between Oct 31 and Nov 4. Batch changes into planned releases (see timeline). No edits to live pages between releases except defect fixes.
8 Medium Internal linking Guides receive little link weight. Header has 47 links, none to guides. /guides is one flat list of 128 links. 28 guides have ≤ 2 contextual inbound links; 3 have none (what-is-an-stl-file, how-to-open-and-view-gcode-files, how-many-metres-in-1kg-filament). Topic sub-hubs on /guides (STL, 3MF, G-code, fit & tolerance, cost). Link each owner page from its tool and 2–3 related guides.
9 Medium Trust signals Nothing says who is behind the site. No About page, no Organization schema, no author or publisher in Article schema. Source citations are good (59 Prusa help links, Marlin, Klipper, 3mf.io). Add an About page (who builds it, how checks work, contact), Organization schema, and publisher on Article schema. Do not invent authors.
10 Med-Low Page speed Every guide page preloads one chunk containing the HTML of all 128 guides. chunk-4zUS4_Ed2.js: 1.2 MB raw, about 300 KB gzipped, referenced by 128 pages. HTML is served uncompressed because the CachingDisabled policy does not negotiate compression. Load guide HTML per route (lazy import per slug). The compression issue is reported only: the project rules forbid cache-policy changes without an explicit request.
11 Low WAF rate limit A WAF block returns 403, which CloudFront maps to 404. If Googlebot ever trips the limit, valid pages look "Not found". Limit 3,000 requests / 5 min per IP across all assets (3d-infra/lib/constructs/cloudfront-web-acl.ts:5); 403→404 mapping in frontend-hosting.ts. Check GSC Crawl stats for 404s on valid URLs and WAF sampled requests for Googlebot. Change only if evidence appears.
12 Low URL variants Trailing-slash and query-string variants return 200 instead of redirecting. /guides/how-to-check-stl-for-errors/ returns 200. Self-canonicals mitigate this. Optional: 301 trailing slash in the CloudFront function. Infra change, needs separate authorization.
13 Low Schema FAQPage schema on 73 pages has no effect. Google limits FAQ rich results to government and health sites since 2023. 73 built pages contain FAQPage. Harmless. Remove if it is maintenance burden; not a priority.
14 Low Utility pages /privacy and /cookies are index, follow but not in the sitemap. Robots meta in built HTML. Decide deliberately; noindex is the usual choice.

Next production update: Release 1 on or after Thursday 2026-12-03.

The last deploy was 2026-11-04. Your SEO strategy requires a full 28-day window after a release before the next broad batch, which ends 2026-12-02. Local preparation can and should happen during the freeze. Defect, broken-tool and factual corrections can still ship earlier.

Decided 2026-11-19

Keep overlapping pages and retarget

Keep both pages in each pair and expand them. Where they compete for one query, give one page a different keyword. No merges or retirements are planned, so no redirect decision is needed now.

Blocking

Printer pages: noindex or invest

Noindex until they carry unique content, or expand them now with content only InspectMesh has, such as fit-checker results on sample models per printer. Spec sheets alone will not compete with manufacturers and review sites. Still open.

Decided 2026-11-19

STL/3MF twin tools: keep both

Keep separate STL and 3MF tool pages and expand both with format-specific content. Where a pair still competes, retarget the 3MF page (see keyword map).

Method

  • Parsed all 221 sitemap URLs from the production build (3d-frontend/dist, built 2026-11-04) for title, description, canonical, robots, H1 count and word counts inside <main>.
  • Near-duplicates: 5-word shingle overlap of main content between every pair of pages.
  • Internal links: links inside <main> of non-hub pages, so header navigation and hub listings are excluded.
  • Live checks with curl against https://www.inspectmesh.com: status codes, redirects, headers, live sitemap.
